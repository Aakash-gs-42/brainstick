Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

In short:

• BrainStick has no accounts and we don't collect your personal information ourselves.

• Your progress stays on your phone.

• Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and purchases are handled by Google Play with RevenueCat. These services process some device data, as described below.

1. Who we are

BrainStick (the "app") is a puzzle game published by G S Aakash, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email brainstick.support@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress only on your device: the levels you've solved, your stars and best times, your daily streak, how many hints you've used, any hints you've bought, whether BrainStick Pro is active, and your sound and vibration settings. We never receive this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app has no sign-in and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional video you can watch to get a hint, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never shown to players who've bought BrainStick Pro). To deliver and measure these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads can be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy choices. If you don't consent, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalized.

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (Google's "PG" rating), and sensitive categories are blocked.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

You can buy BrainStick Pro (a one-time unlock) and hint packs. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your card or bank details.

To confirm purchases, restore them on a new device and handle refunds, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which products you bought and when), an anonymous app user ID that is not linked to your name or email, and basic device information such as platform and app version.

See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy, which covers Google Play.

5. What we don't collect

The app does not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and it does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

6. Children

BrainStick is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, email us and we'll help remove it.

7. How long data is kept

Data stored on your device stays there until you uninstall the app or reset your progress in Settings. Google and RevenueCat keep the information they process for as long as their own policies describe; purchase records are typically kept for as long as needed to provide restores and meet tax and accounting rules.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Ad personalization: reset or delete your advertising ID on Android in Settings → Google → All services → Ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can also use Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • On-device data: use Settings → Reset Progress in the app, or uninstall it.
  • Purchase records: email us to ask for access to, or deletion of, the purchase records associated with your app user ID. We'll pass deletion requests to RevenueCat. Deleting these records means Pro can no longer be restored on a new device.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation, or the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to withdraw consent. We don't sell your personal information. To make a request, email brainstick.support@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Security and international transfers

Data handled by Google and RevenueCat is transmitted over encrypted connections and may be processed on servers outside India, including in the United States, under those companies' safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app or in its Google Play listing.
